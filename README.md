## 🧠 About Me

I'm a **final-year B.E. student in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning** at Poojya Doddappa Appa College of Engineering, Kalaburagi, Karnataka (graduating 2026).

I build systems at the intersection of **deep learning, computer vision, and language models** — from satellite imagery pipelines at ISRO to autonomous campus robots to LLM fine-tuning workflows.

- 🛰️ **ISRO SAC Research Intern** — Built an end-to-end dense matching pipeline on Chandrayaan-2 OHRC lunar stereo imagery using MASt3R (ViT-Large), achieving **237M+ verified sub-pixel correspondences** and **7.5 cm planimetric accuracy**
- 🤖 **ARIA Robot** — Autonomous serving & query robot funded with **₹1.5 Lakhs**, capable of real-time voice conversations and RAG-powered knowledge retrieval
- 🏆 **Google Gen AI Exchange Hackathon** — Top 5 Nationally | Silver Medal at INEX International Event
- 📄 **Published Research** in IJIRT on Advancing AI in Digital Learning

End-to-end deep learning pipeline using **MASt3R (ViT-Large)** on Chandrayaan-2 OHRC stereo imagery (12,000×76,618 px).
